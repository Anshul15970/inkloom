
Inkloom AI Brand Governance Platform
Round 1 Proposal & Technical Architecture Document

Section 1: Understanding of the Given Problem Statement
1.1 The Core Challenge: Velocity vs. Compliance
Modern organizations operate in an ultra-fast publishing environment where digital content is generated simultaneously by internal employees, external marketing agencies, and generative AI platforms. While this decentralized production model enables rapid market reach, it creates an acute operational tension between content velocity and brand compliance.
When publishing speed takes priority over systematic oversight, critical brand errors inevitably slip into public channels. These issues include:
• Factual Inaccuracies & Unsubstantiated Claims: Outdated product numbers, incorrect pricing, or illegal promotional statements that expose the organization to legal liability and brand erosion.
• Inconsistent Messaging & Tone: Disjointed voice across marketing touchpoints that dilutes corporate identity and confuses prospective customers.
• Visual Non-Compliance: Off-brand color palettes, incorrect logo placements, improper aspect ratios, and poor visual accessibility compliance.
1.2 The Bottleneck of Traditional Governance
Existing brand governance approaches fail because they rely on manual "gatekeeper" review models. Human compliance teams become severe operational bottlenecks, forcing creators to either bypass compliance protocols to hit tight deadlines or endure long review delays that destroy content momentum. Conversely, purely automated "pass/fail" filters alienate creators by blanket-rejecting drafts without context or actionable guidance.
1.3 Targeted Problem & Objective
The objective of this solution is to remove the friction between speed and governance by embedding intelligent, explainable brand checks directly into the creator's active workspace prior to publication. Rather than acting as a punitive blocker, the platform balances automated precision with human decision-making—detecting factual, tonal, and visual discrepancies, explaining why an issue was flagged with direct guideline citations, and providing actionable corrections that preserve the creator's original intent.
Core Operational Insight: The platform transforms compliance from an external blocking gate into an in-context assistant, reducing turnaround times from days to seconds while eliminating off-brand risk.

Section 3: Proposed Solution & Value Proposition
3.1 Solution Concept
The proposed platform is a dual-workflow AI brand governance architecture that separates administrative rule management from decentralized creator checks. By uniting a centralized Admin Web Dashboard with a lightweight Browser Extension, the platform establishes a seamless loop between brand policy definition and real-time content enforcement.
3.2 Core Value Proposition
• Velocity Without Risk: Enables creators to publish faster while ensuring 100% adherence to factual claims, visual identity, and tone guidelines.
• Explainable & Prescriptive AI: Replaces silent rejections with clear citations to official guidelines and actionable fix suggestions that retain creator voice.
• Dual-Engine Precision: Separates deterministic mathematical visual checks (hex colors, logo boundaries) from subjective creative evaluations (brand "vibe").
3.3 Key Differentiators & Originality
1. Zero Workflow Interruption: Integrates governance directly into the creator's browser-based publishing tool via a Chrome Extension.
2. Split Vision Engine Architecture: Combines deterministic computer vision for hard visual compliance with vision LLMs for qualitative style checks.
3. Enterprise Privacy Pivot: Features an explicit roadmap transitioning from standard APIs during hackathon MVP to Google Cloud Vertex AI in production to guarantee zero model-training on confidential enterprise data.
Section 4: Complete Product Workflow
Step 1: Rule Ingestion & Asset Indexing (Admin Dashboard)
1. Fact & Policy Setup: Brand Managers log into the Admin Dashboard and upload approved "Golden Facts" in structured formats (CSV/Excel) alongside written guidelines.
2. Vector Indexing: The system processes text documents into a Vector Database (Pinecone / Qdrant) using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) embeddings.
3. Visual Asset Upload: Managers upload approved logos, color swatches, and "Golden Assets" representing ideal brand aesthetics.
Step 2: Content Check Triggering (Chrome Extension)
1. Draft Creation: The creator drafts text and visual media inside their CMS workspace (e.g., WordPress, Webflow, LinkedIn).
2. In-Context Execution: The creator clicks the Chrome Extension to run a pre-publication audit without leaving the CMS interface.
Step 3: Multi-Modal Evaluation Engine
Text Evaluation Pipeline (RAG + Gemini LLM):
The draft text is passed to the vector search engine (Pinecone/Qdrant) to retrieve matching "Golden Facts" and tone rules. Gemini LLM analyzes the text against retrieved contexts, identifying inaccurate claims, tone mismatches, and improper terminology, outputting explicit citations pointing to the exact source rule alongside actionable rewrite suggestions.
Visual Evaluation Pipeline (Split Vision Engine):
• Hard Rules (Deterministic OpenCV): Python scripts extract exact hex color codes from draft visuals and perform template matching for logo placement, ensuring 100% mathematical accuracy.
• Subjective Style (Gemini Pro Vision): Gemini Pro Vision evaluates image aesthetics against uploaded "Golden Assets" using few-shot prompting to judge brand "vibe" and qualitative consistency.
Step 4: Human-in-the-Loop Collaboration & Governance
1. Inline Review: The creator reviews flagged inline warnings directly within the Chrome Extension, accepting automated suggestions or making manual edits.
2. Escalation & Approval: If a check flags a high-risk policy variance, the draft is escalated to the Admin Dashboard review queue for manager sign-off.
3. Continuous Governance Insights: Repeated mistakes or ambiguous guidelines are highlighted in the Admin Dashboard analytics, providing brand managers with data to refine rules over time.
Section 5: Main Planned Features
5.1 Admin Dashboard Capabilities (Brand Managers & Compliance Officers)
• Golden Facts Ingestion: Allows managers to upload structured spreadsheets (CSV/Excel) containing approved facts, pricing, statistics, and product specifications, automatically indexed into a Vector DB.
• Rule & Policy Configuration: Enables custom definition of banned keywords, mandatory legal disclosures, and tone-of-voice parameters across different content channels.
• Visual Golden Assets Repository: Centralized storage for approved logos, exact hex color swatches, typography rules, and reference images representing approved brand aesthetics.
• Review Queue & Escalation Management: A centralized workflow workspace where escalated high-risk drafts are reviewed, commented on, and approved or rejected with audit trails.
• Governance Analytics & Recurring Error Insights: Tracks system usage to identify frequently violated brand rules, flagging areas where guidelines may be unclear or misunderstood.
5.2 Creator Capabilities (Chrome Extension for Content Marketers)
• Native Workspace Integration: Operates directly inside popular browser-based publishing platforms (WordPress, LinkedIn, Webflow, Canva) without requiring external context switching.
• One-Click Pre-Publication Audit: Analyzes text copy and visual media directly from the active CMS page prior to publishing.
• Inline Factual & Claim Verification: Compares draft copy against stored Golden Facts, highlighting outdated numbers or unverified promotional claims alongside direct citations to official source documents.
• Actionable Correction Engine: Replaces punitive rejections with prescriptive, AI-generated rewrites that preserve the creator's original tone while restoring factual accuracy.
• Visual Identity Check: Evaluates uploaded visual assets for color palette accuracy, logo placement, aspect ratio compliance, and overall aesthetic alignment.
• Human-in-the-Loop Feedback & Escalation: Allows creators to accept suggested edits with a single click, provide explanatory notes for exceptions, or request manual review from a manager.

6.3 Technical Highlight: The Split Vision Engine
Visual brand compliance involves two fundamentally different challenges: objective mathematical rules and subjective aesthetic feel. A key architectural innovation is the Split Vision Engine, which decouples deterministic checks from generative AI evaluations.
1. OpenCV for Deterministic Hard Rules (Why Not an LLM?):
• 100% Mathematical Precision: OpenCV extracts exact RGB and Hex values from pixel matrices. LLMs are probabilistic and cannot guarantee exact pixel-level color code accuracy.
• Zero Hallucination Risk: Template matching algorithmically calculates exact bounding box coordinates and aspect ratios without risk of false positives.
• Ultra-Low Latency & Cost Efficiency: OpenCV executes in milliseconds locally on CPU without external API calls, significantly reducing infrastructure costs and response time.
2. Gemini Pro Vision for Subjective Style & Brand "Vibe":
• Few-Shot Visual Prompting: Gemini Pro Vision is provided with uploaded Golden Assets as contextual reference examples.
• Contextual Aesthetic Reasoning: The model evaluates nuanced criteria such as composition, mood, subject relevance, and visual clutter, producing human-readable qualitative explanations.
6.4 Enterprise Privacy & Data Security Roadmap (Google Cloud Vertex AI)
Protecting confidential corporate information—such as unreleased product specifications, financial claims, and proprietary design assets—is a critical governance requirement.
• Hackathon MVP Architecture: The prototype utilizes the standard Gemini API to demonstrate core RAG and vision pipeline capabilities.
• Production Deployment Roadmap (Vertex AI Pivot): For enterprise adoption, the backend will migrate entirely to Google Cloud Vertex AI.
• Zero Model-Training Guarantee: Deploying via Vertex AI ensures customer data, uploaded Golden Facts, and proprietary visual assets are never logged, stored, or used to train public foundation models.
• Enterprise Control & Compliance: Guarantees customer data isolation within private cloud VPC boundaries, supporting HIPAA/SOC2 compliance and strict access controls.

Section 8: Development Plan & Milestone Roadmap
To maximize feasibility within the tight hackathon timeframe, the development plan prioritizes a tightly scoped, high-impact MVP during the event, followed by expanded enterprise capabilities in the post-event roadmap.
8.1 Phase 1: Hackathon MVP (Event Build Window)
Stage 1: Ingestion & Vector Index Setup (Days 1–2)
Build FastAPI backend endpoints to parse uploaded Golden Facts (CSV/Excel) and generate vector embeddings stored in Pinecone/Qdrant. Implement RAG context retrieval pipelines using standard Gemini API.
Stage 2: Core Chrome Extension & Text Verification (Days 2–3)
Construct a lightweight Manifest V3 Chrome Extension that extracts DOM text from active CMS text fields (e.g., WordPress, LinkedIn). Connect extension triggers to the RAG endpoint to perform real-time text analysis, returning inline claim verification, source citations, and suggested edits.
Stage 3: Basic OpenCV Visual Check & Admin UI (Days 3–4)
Implement Python OpenCV scripts to sample uploaded image files and verify exact hex color palette alignment against defined brand swatches. Build a lightweight React Admin Dashboard for uploading brand rules, viewing Golden Facts, and inspecting audit logs.
Stage 4: End-to-End Integration & Refinement (Days 4–5)
Connect extension UI overlays to render color pass/fail status and inline text corrections. Conduct internal testing across sample blog posts and marketing copy to ensure reliable execution within seconds.
8.2 Phase 2: Post-Event Production Roadmap
1. Enterprise Privacy & Security Pivot (Vertex AI): Migrate backend infrastructure from standard Gemini APIs to Google Cloud Vertex AI to guarantee private cloud tenant isolation and zero model-training.
2. Full Split Vision Engine: Expand OpenCV capabilities to include multi-scale template matching for logo boundary verification and deploy Gemini Pro Vision for qualitative visual brand vibe judging.
3. Governance Analytics & Multi-Tenant Support: Integrate role-based access control (RBAC), single sign-on (SSO), and predictive analytics tracking recurring compliance errors across departments.

9.1 Eliminating Manual Review Bottlenecks
• Instant In-Context Feedback: By embedding evaluations directly within the creator's native CMS via a Chrome Extension, compliance checks take seconds rather than days, eliminating long review queues.

• Brand Voice Consistency: Guarantees a unified corporate identity across all publishing channels, protecting brand equity across internal, agency, and AI-generated copy.
